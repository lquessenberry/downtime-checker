WORLD GOVERNMENT AND MILITARY WEB ADDRESSES
===========================================
Generated for IP down checker use. 
Note: Domains are listed (preferred for monitoring as IPs change frequently due to CDNs, load balancing, and updates). 
Resolve domains to current IPs in your checker tool (e.g., via DNS lookup). 
Many sites use HTTPS and may redirect. Data primarily from UN EGOVKB national portals and official sources. 
Military sections include official defense ministries and known public base/installation pages where available. 
Not exhaustive for every agency or base worldwide.

NOTES
===========================================
- Domains ending in .gov, .gov.uk, .gc.ca, .gouv.fr, .go.jp, etc., are typically official.
- For military bases: Most individual base pages are under national defense domains (e.g., .mil for US). Full global list of every base website is not centrally published; use defense ministry sites and official directories.
- Verify current URLs as governments update them. Some listed portals may have changed or be offline.
- For IP resolution in your checker: Use tools like dig, nslookup, or libraries to get A/AAAA records. Note many resolve to CDN IPs (Cloudflare, Akamai, etc.).
- This is a public information compilation for legitimate monitoring purposes. Respect robots.txt and terms of service when accessing.
