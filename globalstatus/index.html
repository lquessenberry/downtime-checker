US pins are counties from utilities that report to DOE/ORNL ODIN, not every US outage. United Kingdom pins are unplanned outages from UK Power Networks, Northern Powergrid, and National Grid Electricity Distribution. SP Energy Networks, Electricity North West, Northern Ireland Electricity, and SSEN are not included. Canada pins are Hydro-Québec unplanned outages only. Australia, Ireland, New Zealand, and South Africa do not publish an open live location feed that can be pinned.
